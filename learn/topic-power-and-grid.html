Plain-English glossary

Transformer
Equipment that steps voltage up or down so electricity can move across the grid. Large power transformers take years to order and deliver.
Switchgear
Equipment that connects, protects and isolates electrical circuits in a substation or inside a data centre.
Interconnection queue
The waiting list of power plants and large users asking to connect to the grid.
Power purchase agreement (PPA)
A long-term contract to buy electricity at an agreed price.
Restart and uprate
Bringing a shut-down reactor back into service, and raising the output of one that is already running.
HALEU
High-assay low-enriched uranium, a fuel many next-generation reactor designs need.
Critical path
The longest chain of dependent steps in a project. It sets the finish date, whatever the other steps are doing.
