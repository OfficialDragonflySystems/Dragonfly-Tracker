Plain-English glossary

Co-packaged optics
Putting the parts that turn electricity into light on the same package as the chip, so data travels as light from the start.
Indium phosphide (InP)
A crystal used to make the lasers in optical links. Very few firms grow it, on small wafers.
Transceiver
A module that sends and receives data over a link, converting between electrical and optical signals.
Single point of failure
One supplier or step that everything else depends on, so losing it stops the whole chain.

The question still open

Unresolved. Can indium-phosphide laser supply grow fast enough, or does it set how quickly optical links can be deployed?
