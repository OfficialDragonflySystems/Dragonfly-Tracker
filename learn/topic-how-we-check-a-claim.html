Plain-English glossary

Primary source
The original document or dataset (a filing, a report) rather than someone's summary of it.
Secondhand figure
A number reported from a source we have not seen ourselves. We label it and keep it out of graded cells.
Pre-registration
Writing the rule for a test down before looking at the data.
Hindsight (look-ahead) bias
Using information that was not available at the time to judge a past decision.
Invalidation print
The specific number that, if it appears, shows a thesis is wrong. We write it down in advance.

The question still open

Unresolved. What would make us drop our own headline conclusion? We publish that on the scoreboard.
