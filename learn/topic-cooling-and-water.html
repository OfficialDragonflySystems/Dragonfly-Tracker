Plain-English glossary

Water withdrawal
Water taken from a source. Some is returned.
Water consumption
Water that is not returned to the source, for example because it evaporates.
Evaporative cooling
Cooling by evaporating water. Effective, and it consumes water.
Cold-plate (direct-to-chip) cooling
Liquid circulated through plates attached to the chips.
Immersion cooling
Submerging hardware in a non-conductive liquid.
Waste heat
Low-grade heat a data centre rejects. It can warm greenhouses or buildings nearby.

The question still open

Unresolved. How much water do data centres use where water is actually scarce, as opposed to national totals? Our water comparison says a national total cannot answer that.
