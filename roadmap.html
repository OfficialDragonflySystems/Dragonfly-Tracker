Roadmap · last updated 18 September 2026

What exists, what we have committed to, and what we are only thinking about.

Three sections, in that order, and nothing else. If a feature is not in the first section, you are not paying for it.

The rule

No feature is advertised before it works.

Everything under “Available now” is something we have checked end to end: the code runs, the data flows, and a subscriber can open it today. Anything that is built but has no data behind it, or that we could not verify ourselves, does not appear there — not in a lighter font, not with an asterisk, not at all. This works in both directions: if something on that list stops working, it comes off the page, and the removal goes in the changelog with the date.

What we removed, and why. Until 18 September 2026 our homepage carried a roadmap that unlocked features at subscriber counts — sector filters at 10 subscribers, earnings-call summaries further up, a B2B API further up still, broker connections “as we grow”. We deleted it. A milestone tied to a subscriber number tells you how badly we want your money, not when you will get the thing, and it quietly made every unbuilt feature look like your fault for not recruiting fast enough. Dates and prerequisites we control replace it. This page is the replacement, and it is shorter on purpose.

2 · Committed next

Three commitments, with dates you can hold us to.

Three, not thirty. Each has a date or a stated prerequisite, and each is written so that on the date you can look at the site and decide for yourself whether we did it. If we miss one, it gets a dated entry in the changelog saying we missed it.

  • 1. Close the three blank baselines on the scoreboard — one source a month
    Three of the five physical tells on the scoreboard carry no figure of ours: grid interconnection queues, memory and optics pricing, and data-centre starts. They sit under “Baselines we are still building” rather than as empty rows, because an empty row still reads like a reading. We will publish one of them a month, each with its primary source, the date the figure refers to, and the basis it is measured on — and we will not borrow a number from somewhere else to fill a row.
    Grid interconnection queues by 31 October 2026 · memory and optics pricing by 30 November 2026 · data-centre starts by 31 December 2026.
    How to check: open the scoreboard on 1 November, 1 December and 1 January and count how many of the five physical tells carry a dated figure of ours. It should read three, then four, then five.
  • 2. A “verify this claim” page behind every published figure
    Anyone can already verify a signal’s signature at /verify. Editorial figures do not have that: a footnote takes you to a source list, not to a record of what we measured. We will extend the same machinery to figures, so a number resolves to a page showing the primary source, the date the figure refers to, the date we last checked it, and any correction applied since.
    Every figure on the scoreboard by 31 December 2026; every figure in Learn articles published from 1 January 2027 onward, from the day each is published.
    How to check: click any figure on the scoreboard after 31 December 2026. If it does not land on a per-figure record, we did not do it.
  • 3. Compound’s scoring loop — one line of reasoning in, a score out
    Compound already stores a time horizon and a sell thesis with each position, but the loop is open: nothing goes back and marks what happened when that horizon arrives. We will capture one line of reasoning at the moment of the decision and score that entry automatically when its horizon passes — was the reason still the reason, and did it play out — so the journal can tell you which of your own reasons work.
    Capture and automatic scoring shipped by 31 March 2027. Prerequisite before we publish any calibration number out of it: at least 30 entries whose horizon has passed, because a hit rate on fewer than that is noise, and we are not going to do to you what we refuse to do to ourselves.
    How to check: after 31 March 2027, a Premium account’s Compound tab should show a scored / not-yet-scored count on its own entries.
3 · Exploring

Ideas. Not promises.

Nothing in this section is promised. There are no dates here, no prerequisites, and no implied delivery. None of it should affect your decision to subscribe or to stay subscribed — if anything below is the reason you are considering a subscription, please do not subscribe yet. One or two have had a single trial run; most will never ship, and the ones that do not will simply disappear from this list without ceremony. If one does ship, it moves up to “Available now” and appears in the changelog on the day it does.
  • Said vs Happened
    A dated record of what prominent public forecasters actually said, set against what actually happened — the same standard we apply to our own calls, applied to theirs.
  • What the World Pays
    Per-country comparisons of what a household really pays for the same basic thing, at the price they meet rather than the headline rate. One trial dossier exists, on fuel. It is not published.
  • How the World Covered It
    One event read through the outlets of many countries at once, to show where the accounts diverge and where they agree. One trial run exists, across 23 outlets in 14 countries. It is not published.
  • Broker statement import for Compound
    Upload or paste a broker statement instead of typing trades in by hand. To be explicit, because our old roadmap was not: this would be file import, not account linking, and we have no plan to connect to your brokerage.
