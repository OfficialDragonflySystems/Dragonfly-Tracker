The log

From 1 September 2026.

One line per change, newest first. Drafts and internal work are not listed — only things a reader or a subscriber can see. Entries marked in gold are fixes to something we got wrong. What comes next is on the roadmap.

18 Sep 2026
  • The homepage is now one short page instead of 233KB of sales copy; the old version is archived, not deleted. See it
  • The weekly scoreboard came out of draft and is now public. Open it
  • Deleted every tier claim the code could not back: the 60-day Premium trial, exit-strategy guidance, the monthly recap, the macro bar, the private community, the weekly Q&A, and “re-evaluated every 15 minutes” on Radar, which has been hourly since 24 August.
  • Closed the paid-data leaks. The Distillery served its whole page to anyone behind a CSS overlay, the live signal’s ticker and its conviction score were readable without an account, and real-time alerts sold as Investor were reaching Basic. All four are now enforced on the server.
  • Our own 11 September track-record correction had never reached the homepage, which was still showing the old numbers in seven places, including the headline. An outside review caught it. Fixed.
  • The weekly digest moved from Sunday to Friday — the day we advertise — and now always carries the week’s published research and the scoreboard, so it is not empty in a quiet week.
  • Scoreboard: rows that carry a figure now come first, there is a column for what changed since last week, and each source links to the filing rather than to a site summarising it.
17 Sep 2026
  • First version of the weekly scoreboard, published unlisted so it could be reviewed before anyone was pointed at it.
  • Stopped selling exit-strategy guidance. Our own research found that trade management gave back more than it saved, and the onboarding email said so while the pricing card sold the opposite.
  • Published llms.txt on the public site. It had been a 404, so tools reading the site could not find the research library at all.
16 Sep 2026
  • Published the three-month re-score of the June AI-bubble tests, including the two things the June piece missed. Read it
  • America’s Trade: dated corrections added after outside review, with every remaining point re-checked against its source. Read it
12 Sep 2026
  • Published the piece explaining the track-record correction: what the numbers were, what they should have been, and the arithmetic. Read it
11 Sep 2026
  • Corrected the published track record downward: cumulative +380% became +343.5%, the S&P comparison +117% became +109.6%, and the win rate 83% became 82.8%. Every figure now carries the date it was measured and the average holding period behind it. See the page
  • The Decision Ledger’s headline number had been generated once in June and served as current ever since. Re-run, re-dated, and the stale-by-design part removed.
10 Sep 2026
  • Published a machine-readable index of the research library, which had been invisible to anything that was not a human clicking through. Browse it
4 Sep 2026
  • Published two deep dives: The Debt Machine and America’s Trade.
  • Added a freshness check after both had sat finished but unpublished, because nothing was watching for that.
3 Sep 2026
  • The Debt Machine: eight factual errors found in an outside review and fixed before publication, with the mechanism and the counter-argument added.
This log starts on 1 September 2026, which is when we started keeping it. Corrections to published articles also live in the corrections log, where they sit beside the claim they replace and are never removed.
